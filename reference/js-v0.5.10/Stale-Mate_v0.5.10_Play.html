Stale-Mate v0.5.10

Every piece has a life. Every decision has a cost.

Traditional · deputy and middle managers — King → Queen → 2 Rooks, 2 Knights and 2 Bishops → 2 end-to-end Pawn-workers. Starting organization only: no artificial CP discount or management aura yet.
Skills update: Starting skill slots P/N/B/R/Q/K = 0/1/1/2/3/2. Pawns gain one slot and choose one of three skills at levels 3, 6, 9, … . Click Skills ready after a milestone. Skill effects are stated explicitly; route disclosure remains a design experiment. Learning is free; active skills use CP and an activation. Existing movement and work rules remain. Pawn Workers: Organization choice changes the starting roster: Traditional K+Q+2R+2N+2B+2P; Middle K+2N+6P; Flatten K+9P. No independent engineer. Delegation/management overhead remains a future experiment, not an unannounced free bonus. Training camps are UNLIMITED for now; no new cap has been imposed. Rook 2 CP · Bishop 1 CP · Knight/Pawn/King 1 CP · Queen 3 CP (2 in original mode). Default budget stays 3 CP. Pawns move one square in all three forward directions, but capture only on the two forward diagonals. Worker ladder: Lv1 forest clearing; Lv2 bridges + watchtowers; Lv3 mountain clearing; Lv4 training camps. Pawns remain capturable and use normal activation CP. Camps reveal eight neighboring squares; a friendly pawn stationed there adds the outer ring. Watchtowers reveal eight neighbors. Terrain blocks extended sight. Pawn camp production: sacrifice the Lv4+ operator for 2 friendly work turns, or keep it stationed for 5. Choose 2 level-1 pawns, 1 knight or 1 bishop; no extra King XP. Building the camp takes 3 work turns before recruitment. No free training camps by default; a level-4 pawn must build the first camp. Optional Quick Start retains one ready camp per side. King XP recruitment becomes available after a camp is built (1 CP). The engineer remains removed. Fog memory, one activation per piece, sole-royal king capture, optional shield hits/Fission and the 20-unit cap remain. Old command-camp saves import under the updated rules without resetting your remaining CP or used activations.
