# Unity licensing checked 2026-10-30

Official sources:
- Unity: https://unity.com/products/pricing-updates
- Unity Software Terms: https://unity.com/pages/license-compliance
- Unity plan details: https://unity.com/products
- Unreal: https://www.unrealengine.com/license

Unity **Personal** is free for eligible users whose relevant Total Finances do not exceed **US$200,000 in the most recent 12-month period**. That is not a $1M *profit* threshold; it concerns relevant gross revenue/funding, with definitions varying for individuals, companies and clients. Pro is required above the threshold; Unity's 2026 advertised plan was **US$2,310/year per seat** if prepaid annually (or US$210/mo per seat). Above $25M, Enterprise is generally required. Unity's proposed Runtime Fee was canceled; there is no new per-install fee. The Unity made-with splash screen is optional in Unity 6.

The frequently quoted **US$1M lifetime gross revenue** royalty exclusion belongs to **Unreal Engine's typical game royalty license**, not Unity. Unreal generally charges 5% of applicable product revenue above the $1M exclusion, subject to license exceptions.

These are general engine-plan terms, **not an individualized legal determination** about a publisher, company-owned work, employer IP ownership, or license eligibility. Verify the current legally binding terms at time of use/release.
