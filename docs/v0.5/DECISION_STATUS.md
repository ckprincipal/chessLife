# ChessLife v0.5 decision status — 2026-10-29

## Accepted product direction
ChessLife is the working title. Default 50×50 board with map/army options. Each piece has 99 levels, with **qualitative transformations** rather than incremental HP/damage inflation. Explore via three-state, non-permanent fog (never explored black; remembered but currently unseen mist; currently observed clear). Mist allows **speculative movement** that stops just before concealed blockage and consumes an action; unexplored black blocks orders. Chess-like piece geometry, capped long sliding rays, no stalemate, king capture wins. King is only royal and a 1.5× XP summoner/commander. Queen starts strongest and grows slowly into exclusive War, Phantom or Choice/Blood (decisions, tradeoffs, sacrifice vs mercy and nonlethal alternatives). K/Q skill slots=3, R/B/N=2, pawn=0 initially and learns. Pawn careers and relationships matter; first team discovery gives +0.30 XP once, not repeatable through mist.

## Present but newly questioned
- v0.4 full villager production/resources: **experimental, not core** (user concerned about complexity).
- v0.4 granular numeric HP: **experimental, not core** (user concerned about complexity).

## New promising experiments (not final balance rules)
- Some selected units may need up to **three legal capture hits** to defeat (shield pips, no large numeric HP bar). Not universal/automatic from levels.
- High-level eligible pieces may **split into two**, source and new unit BOTH become level 1; veteran skills/protections lost. Prevent duplicating king, high-value queen multiplication and XP/lineage farming. Fission as a consequential decision.
- Campaign-persistent characters and injuries not yet decided.

Keep old mechanics available as controlled comparisons; do not declare a new combat/economy default without evidence and user approval.
